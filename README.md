​Activity Summary:
Completed a 45-minute hands-on PC disassembly and reassembly session guided by UTM lab technicians, followed by a 15-minute quiz on hardware components, tool usage, and assembly sequences. Because 6 PCs were provided for the session, members of Group 7 joined Groups 1 to 6 so every student could participate hands-on and take group documentation photos together.  

​My Contribution:
Collaborated with my teammates to unscrew the chassis, identify internal hardware components (motherboard, CPU and cooling fan, RAM modules, PSU, and storage drive), reconnect power and data cables in sequence, and secure the system unit.  

​Reflection:
Working with physical desktop components bridged classroom computer architecture theory with practical hardware maintenance. Coordinating around a compact casing taught our combined group how to divide steps clearly and double-check connections before powering on. Next time, I will map out the front-panel and SATA cable layouts prior to disassembly to improve our speed and accuracy.
